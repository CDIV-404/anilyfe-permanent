# Marketplace preview notes

The desktop preview confirms the supplied marketplace composition is implemented as a light dashboard: a two-tier search and utility header, category rail, central cinematic hero, circular category shortcuts, product grid, 3D preview panel, deals module, and community/seller rail. The product images, hero visual, and existing interactive GLB preview render in the storefront.

The mobile preview confirms that the header collapses to the wordmark and menu control, the marketplace content flows into one clear column, and the account, seller-application, and administrator pages remain readable and usable at a 390-pixel viewport. No layout clipping was visible in the captured pages.
